About BrewAI

BrewAI is a research project developed at Anglia Ruskin University (ARU), in collaboration with EIT Food and supported by Innovate UK ICURe. Working directly with breweries and beverage producers, we are building practical tools to measure, understand, and reduce the carbon footprint of production — at every scale.

BrewAI™ is our attempt to make low-carbon brewing practical at every scale. We capture production-linked energy data, translate it into clear carbon insight, and then use analytics and AI to surface opportunities to reduce emissions and cost.

Read the longer story

This is a university-led research project with real-world impact. Led by Anglia Ruskin University and backed by Innovate UK ICURe and EIT Food, BrewAI is building an intelligent network of beverage producers of all sizes — collecting real-time energy and carbon data, spotting patterns across equipment, process steps, seasons and tariffs, and turning those patterns into practical decisions.

From measurement to action: our platform helps producers track emissions in near real-time, forecast cleaner production timing, and evaluate whether interventions actually worked — using academic rigour rather than assumptions.

Privacy is core, not an afterthought. Breweries remain in control of their data. Benchmarking is designed to be secure so participants can learn from the network without exposing commercially sensitive detail.

We are iterating directly with producers. As an active research project, we invite beverage makers to help shape what “useful” means in practice — your involvement directly informs the research.
